# Dillon Ultimate Winter Client 008

Windows x64 launcher for Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.267.

Revision 8 is the production winter expansion: 94 managed NeoForge mods, Fresh Animations, and five shader packs. It adds restrained exploration, building, storage, travel, winter ambience, animation, sound, and quality-of-life improvements while preserving the existing world and vanilla-survival balance.

Launcher 6 adds Low, Balanced, High, and Cinematic graphics presets plus a hardware-based recommendation. The signed updater installs, replaces, removes, and repairs managed content without touching Microsoft accounts, saves, screenshots, controls, or other unmanaged personal files.

The dedicated server remains at `schmidt-nan.tun.ply.gg`. Proximity voice uses the managed Simple Voice Chat client and the dedicated Playit UDP tunnel configured by the server.

Download only the signed launcher/update channel. Never provide your Microsoft credentials to anyone; sign in through the Prism Launcher account interface.
